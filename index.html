How it works

Dinner, decided in a minute

No group chat debates, no “I'm fine with anything.” Just swipe.

1

Start a group

Choose restaurants or dishes and how far you'll go. Share the 4-letter code, or send a link that drops friends right in.

2

Everyone swipes

Right for yum, left for nah. Each phone gets the spots in its own shuffled order, so nobody sways anybody.

3

Eat where you match

When everyone likes a place, it's a match. See the favorites, then tap for hours, the menu and directions.

Why Forked

Built for how friends actually pick

✅

Hand-checked spots

Every Upper Valley listing was reviewed by hand, so no pharmacies, caterers or delivery-only brands sneak into your deck.

🍜

Restaurants or cravings

Not sure where? Swipe on pizza, pho or tacos instead, and Forked shows the nearby places that serve your match.

🌲

Local first

Diners, cafés, pubs and creemee stands come before chains. Flip on “Local spots only” to skip chains entirely.

🕒

Hours, menus, directions

Cards show what's open now, and each match links to its menu, website, phone and directions.

📶

Phones talk directly

Your group connects over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, made for when you're all in the same room or car.

🙅

No sign-ups

No accounts, no passwords, no ads. Type your first name and start swiping.

FAQ

Good questions

When can I get Forked?

Forked is in testing now and is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.

Does everyone need the app?

Yes. Each person swipes on their own iPhone. Friends join with your link or 4-letter code, and need to be nearby with Wi-Fi and Bluetooth turned on.

What counts as a match?

A place everyone in the group liked. If nothing gets a unanimous yes, Forked still ranks the spots with the most likes so you can settle it fast.

Does it work outside the Upper Valley?

Yes. Outside the area, Forked finds restaurants near you with Apple Maps. Inside it, you get the hand-checked guide.

A place is missing or wrong. How do I tell you?

Email universe@kportal.net with the place's name and town. The Upper Valley guide is checked by hand, so fixes get looked at by a person.

What happens to my location?

It's only used to pick the search area, which the phones in your group see. There are no Forked accounts or servers. Read the privacy policy.
