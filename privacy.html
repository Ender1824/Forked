Privacy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Forked has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no tracking. What you do in the app stays on your phone and the phones in your group.

What Forked uses

  • Your first name. You type it so friends know who's in the group. It's saved on your phone and shown to the people in your group.
  • Your location, only if you allow it. It's used to center the search area for a round. The search area is shared with the phones in your group so everyone sees the same places. Forked doesn't track your location in the background.
  • Your swipes. Likes are shared with the phones in your group to find matches, and are cleared when the group ends.

How group phones connect

Phones in a group talk to each other directly over Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, using Apple's peer-to-peer networking. Group information isn't sent to or stored on any Forked server.

Places, photos and websites

Restaurant listings come from a guide built into the app and from Apple Maps. To show photos, menus and descriptions, Forked may load a restaurant's own public website, and Apple's Look Around imagery, directly from your phone. Those sites and Apple receive a normal web request from your device, as they would if you visited them in a browser, and their own privacy policies apply.

Website details are cached on your phone so they load faster next time.

What Forked doesn't do

  • No account sign-up, email or phone number.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your information with anyone.

Children

Forked isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Contact

Questions about privacy or anything else? Email universe@kportal.net.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
